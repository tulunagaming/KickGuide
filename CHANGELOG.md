# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First public version.

- Important casters per dungeon for Midnight Season 2 (8 dungeons)
- Casts with description, skull for important casts, Kick / No kick from game data
- Stun information from Mythic Dungeon Tools (3 dungeons) and your own right-click marks
- Important flags stored outside instances, used inside dungeons
- Dungeon map per floor with spawn points, follows your floor inside the dungeon
- Resizable window, compact list, shift-drag to move, `/kg reset`
- MDT button when Mythic Dungeon Tools is installed
- English and German
