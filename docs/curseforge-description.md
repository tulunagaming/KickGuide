# KickGuide

**Know what to kick before the pull.**

KickGuide is a Mythic+ preparation guide for Midnight. Pick a dungeon and see every trash mob that has a cast Blizzard marks as *important* — and what you can do about it.

## What you see

- **Important casters** of the dungeon, bosses in their own group
- For each mob: its casts with description, a **skull** on the important ones and
  - **Kick** — interruptible
  - **No kick · Stun** — cannot be kicked, but the mob can be stunned
  - **No kick · stun immune** — neither, dodge or survive it
- A **map** of every floor with one point per mob group

## Built for the run

- Stays open: resize it, shift-drag it, Esc does not close it
- Compact list mode: portrait and skull count only
- Inside a dungeon it switches to that dungeon and follows your floor
- Blizzard hides the *important* flag inside instances — KickGuide stores it while you are outside, no need to open the window first

## Your own notes

Right-click a mob to mark it as stunnable or stun immune. Your marks are saved.

## Commands

`/kg` open · `/kg minimap` minimap button · `/kg reset` reset window · `/kg map` map check
